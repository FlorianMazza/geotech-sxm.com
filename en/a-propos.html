About us

Field expertise,
the rigor of the trade.

Geotech Caraïbes is the story of a construction professional deeply rooted in his home ground. For more than ten years, David Pivetta and his crews have shaped the soil of Saint-Martin with unwavering operational standards and an intimate knowledge of a territory unlike any other in the world.

WHO WE ARE

A local company,
a recognized expertise.

SAS GEOTECH CARAÏBES is an earthworks and site utilities (VRD) company based in Saint-Martin (97150), working across the entire island — both the French side and the Dutch side (Sint Maarten). Founded by David Pivetta, it was built on simple values: knowledge of the ground, disciplined execution, closeness to clients, and keeping our commitments.

Backed by more than ten years of local earthworks and site utilities experience — carried by David Pivetta and his crews — Geotech Caraïbes has built a diverse portfolio of completed projects — private villas, residential subdivisions, hotels, industrial zones, municipal roadways, utility networks — that reflects its ability to meet the technical demands of the most complex projects in the particular context of Saint-Martin.

SAINT-MARTIN, OUR GROUND

Knowledge
earned in the field.

Working in Saint-Martin can't be improvised. The soil, the climate, the logistical constraints, and the administrative realities of the island demand knowledge earned in the field — not from a textbook.

I.

Detailed knowledge of the soil

Volcanic terrain, clay soils, coastal zones with a near-surface water table: every part of Saint-Martin has its own characteristics. Ten years of projects have allowed us to build a mental map of the island's soils that no outside player can reproduce. That knowledge is built directly into our studies and our execution methodology.

II.

Mastery of the island context

Tropical climate, hurricane season from June to November, material-supply constraints, the dual French-Dutch administration: we factor these realities in from the planning stage. Our jobsites are organized to absorb the unexpected events unique to Saint-Martin without compromising schedules or the quality of the work.

III.

A local network

Trusted local suppliers, utility operators, government offices, engineering firms, architects: Geotech Caraïbes relies on a solid professional network, patiently built over the years. This network is a resource we can put to work directly for our clients to speed up procedures and resolve complex situations.

OUR OPERATIONAL APPROACH

A clear method,
from the first dig to delivery.

Every Geotech Caraïbes project follows a structured five-step process that ensures quality of execution, compliance with the specifications, and client satisfaction.

01

Site study

A site visit by David Pivetta, soil reconnaissance, topographic reading, and identification of specific constraints. A rigorous analysis that determines whether any technical solution is sound.

02

Technical scoping & quote

Defining the execution methodology, the detailed schedule, and the equipment to mobilize. A transparent quote, with no surprises, delivered within 48 hours of the site visit.

03

Controlled execution

Site management by trained professionals, continuous checks on grades and quality, and safety maintained day to day. Operational discipline is our signature.

04

Monitoring & coordination

Regular reporting, progress reviews with the client, and continuous dialogue. You are kept informed of your project's progress at every key stage.

05

Compliant delivery

A joint handover inspection, a final quality check, and a complete as-built package handed to the client. Work delivered on time, in line with the plans and the contractual commitments.
