Landscaping and outdoor works in Saint-Martin: from foundation to finish.

Outdoor works are the visible culmination of a construction or renovation project. They transform bare or functional ground into a space for living, working, or welcoming that reflects the quality and identity of a project. In Saint-Martin, where the aesthetics of outdoor spaces are especially prized — prestige villas, luxury hotels, high-end residences — outdoor works are far more than a finishing touch: they are the natural extension of the architecture.

Geotech Caraïbes handles outdoor works following or alongside its earthworks and site utilities and roadworks (VRD) job sites, ensuring continuity of service and technical consistency across all outdoor spaces. Our detailed knowledge of the Saint-Martin territory — its local materials, its well-adapted plant species, its climatic constraints — is a decisive asset for durable, aesthetically successful projects.

Terraces and outdoor living spaces

The terrace is a central space in Saint-Martin architecture, where outdoor living is everywhere. Geotech Caraïbes builds terraces of every size and style:

  • Exposed-aggregate concrete terraces: a clean, hard-wearing, low-maintenance surface, ideal for modern spaces around pools and villas
  • Tile or natural-stone terraces: premium finishes, with materials selected for their resistance to UV and sea salt
  • Tropical hardwood or composite terraces: natural warmth and tropical aesthetics, with treatment suited to Saint-Martin's humid climate
  • Pedestal pavers: lightweight, ventilated solutions, ideal for roof terraces and spaces built over a slab

Paving and walkways

Access driveways, pedestrian paths, and paved areas structure outdoor spaces and contribute to a project's visual identity. Geotech Caraïbes masters the installation of:

  • Concrete pavers: robust, available in many formats and colors, easily replaced when work is needed on underground networks
  • Natural-stone pavers: limestone, granite, basalt — noble materials for walkways with character
  • Stamped concrete paving: customizable aesthetics at a controlled cost, suited to large areas
  • Stabilized gravel: a permeable, natural solution that allows stormwater infiltration
  • Colored asphalt: for interior driveways, parking areas, and circulation spaces with high durability requirements

Retaining walls and fencing structures

Saint-Martin is an island of pronounced relief. Retaining walls are essential structures for creating usable spaces on sloping ground and for protecting buildings against erosion and landslides.

Geotech Caraïbes builds:

  • Reinforced concrete retaining walls: long-lasting structures, sized for earth pressures and the seismic loads specific to the West Indies
  • Formwork-block or rendered cinder-block walls: economical solutions for moderate heights
  • Gabions: metal cages filled with stone, natural aesthetics, excellent durability in a corrosive environment
  • Dry-stone walls: a reinterpreted traditional technique, with optimal landscape integration
  • Fencing and gates: concrete, galvanized-metal, or treated-wood fencing, sliding or swing gates with motorization

Green spaces and landscaping

Landscaping is inseparable from quality of life in Saint-Martin. Geotech Caraïbes carries out the preparatory work needed to create durable green spaces:

  • Earthworks and preparation of planted areas: topsoil stripping, addition of topsoil, drains where needed
  • Lawn establishment: seeding or laying rolls of turf suited to the tropical climate (St. Augustine grass, Bermuda grass)
  • Planting of trees and beds: species suited to Saint-Martin's climate and salinity, selected for their hurricane resistance
  • Automatic irrigation systems: buried irrigation networks, timers, drip lines for beds
  • Swales and rain gardens: stormwater management through vegetated solutions integrated into the landscape

Specifics of outdoor works in a tropical setting

Resistance to UV and sea salt: the materials used for outdoor works in Saint-Martin must withstand an aggressive combination of intense UV, high humidity, and sea salt. Geotech Caraïbes selects materials and treatments specifically suited to this environment — a guarantee of long-term durability.

Stormwater management: every impervious surface must be properly drained. Our outdoor works systematically incorporate rainwater-collection systems — gutters, drain inlets, swales — sized for Saint-Martin's rainfall intensities.

Hurricane resistance: furnishings, fencing, and plantings must be designed to withstand hurricane-force winds. Our trees are selected for their deep rooting, our fences are anchored in concrete foundations, and our masonry structures are sized for extreme wind loads.

Tropical aesthetics: Saint-Martin is an island of exceptional beauty. Our outdoor works fit into this natural setting through choices of materials and plants that enhance the tropical identity of the place, without artifice.

An offering that complements our VRD expertise

The outdoor works carried out by Geotech Caraïbes are the natural continuation of our site utilities and roadworks (VRD) and platform services. By entrusting the entire cycle — earthworks, networks, platform, outdoor works — to a single contact, you benefit from total technical consistency and simplified coordination of the parties involved.

It is this sense of end-to-end service, from the first pass of topsoil stripping to the delivery of landscape finishes, that sets Geotech Caraïbes apart in the Saint-Martin market.

Frequently asked questions
about outdoor works in Saint-Martin.

Which materials should you choose for a terrace in Saint-Martin?

In Saint-Martin, terrace materials must withstand intense UV, tropical humidity, and sea salt. Exposed-aggregate concrete and porcelain stoneware tile are excellent choices for their durability and low maintenance. Composite decking holds up better than natural wood to swings in humidity and UV. Natural stone (limestone, granite) brings premium aesthetics but requires a suitable water-repellent treatment. Geotech Caraïbes advises you on the ideal material based on your budget, your aesthetics, and your terrace's exposure.

How do you size a retaining wall in Saint-Martin?

Sizing a retaining wall in Saint-Martin must account for earth pressures (depending on the soil type — clay, sand, fill), the loads applied at the top (building, vehicles), seismic loads (a moderate seismicity zone in the West Indies), and hurricane wind loads. For heights greater than 1.50 m, a stability study by a structural engineering firm is generally required. Geotech Caraïbes carries out the work based on the plans validated by the engineering firm.

Which plants are suited to outdoor works in Saint-Martin?

Saint-Martin's vegetation must be selected for its resistance to heat, drought (in the dry season), sea salt, and hurricane winds. Recommended species include: frangipani, hibiscus, bougainvillea, native palms, coconut trees (with reinforced anchoring), treated oleanders, heliconia, and bromeliads. Invasive species such as schefflera or unpruned flamboyant should be avoided near structures. Geotech Caraïbes draws on its network of local landscapers for suitable recommendations.

How do you manage stormwater runoff on a terrace in Saint-Martin?

Every terrace must be designed with adequate slopes (a minimum of 1.5%) for the rapid drainage of rainwater. The collected water is channeled toward gutters, drain inlets, or perimeter swales, then connected to the stormwater (EP) network or to an infiltration system where the soil permits. In Saint-Martin, high rainfall intensities call for generously sized collection sections — a consideration Geotech Caraïbes systematically factors in from the design stage.

Do you carry out outdoor works for hotels and resorts in Saint-Martin?

Yes, Geotech Caraïbes works on hotel and tourism projects in Saint-Martin. These job sites call for careful finishes, strict adherence to deadlines (often constrained by the tourist seasons), and coordination with other trades (architects, landscapers, electricians). Our organization and responsiveness allow us to meet the requirements of the island's high-end projects.

Do you work on renovation projects for existing outdoor spaces?

Yes, Geotech Caraïbes carries out complete renovations of outdoor spaces: repair of cracked or subsided terraces, replacement of aging surfaces, restoration of deteriorated retaining walls, renovation of internal roadways. These interventions on occupied sites require particular care to limit disruption and preserve what is already in place. Our field expertise is a decisive asset for this type of job site.
