No sewer connection? Two areas of expertise. One complete solution.

Not every property in Saint-Martin is served by a public sewer system. For a remote villa, a residential development beyond the sewer network, or a facility in a rural area, an onsite wastewater treatment system treats wastewater directly on the property, with capacity tailored to the site and its use.

This work brings together civil construction and wastewater treatment. That is why Geotech Caraïbes partners with Guamikeni, a recognized Caribbean specialist, to deliver a complete service from design through ongoing maintenance.

Our Partner

Guamikeni — Water Management Company specializes in decentralized wastewater collection and treatment. Based in Saint-Martin / Sint Maarten and Antigua, the company serves clients throughout the Caribbean. Its mission: “Protect Caribbean land and water”.

A member of the Caribbean Water & Wastewater Association (CWWA), Guamikeni designs environmentally responsible solutions for hotels, residential communities, villas, industrial facilities, and marinas.

ON THE JOB

From excavation to completion.

Earthwork & Installation

Preparing the site, installing the system

Every installation starts with excavation, bedding, and leveling. The treatment tanks are then lowered into place by crane and precisely positioned and secured, even on the steep sites commonly found across the island.

The service life of a wastewater system depends on the quality of its civil works. That is where the Geotech Caraïbes team excels.

The Treatment System

A complete treatment process

Trenching, equipment alignment, and pipe connections: each component is installed in sequence and checked before backfilling.

Guamikeni sizes the complete system to match the project's actual wastewater flow and soil conditions, ensuring compliance and lasting performance.

After Completion

Designed to blend into the landscape

Once the work is complete, only the inspection covers remain accessible for maintenance. The system is concealed beneath the restored grounds, ready to provide lasting protection for the lagoon, groundwater, and coastline.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about onsite wastewater treatment.

What is the difference between a public sewer connection and onsite wastewater treatment?

A public sewer system carries wastewater from your property to a centralized treatment plant. An onsite wastewater treatment system treats wastewater directly on your property using a self-contained system when a public sewer connection is not available. For public sewer systems, visit our sewer services page.

Why partner with Guamikeni for onsite wastewater treatment?

Onsite wastewater treatment requires two areas of expertise: civil construction — excavation, installation, and connections — which is Geotech Caraïbes' core business; and wastewater treatment engineering — system sizing, equipment, commissioning, and maintenance — which is Guamikeni's specialty. By bringing both together, we provide a single point of contact for a reliable, compliant system with ongoing support.

Can you upgrade an existing septic system?

Yes. We upgrade or replace outdated or noncompliant systems for homes, villas, and facilities open to the public. We assess the existing installation, recommend a suitable treatment system, and carry out the work.

Do you work in Sint Maarten and on other islands?

Yes. Geotech Caraïbes works across the entire island, on both the French and Dutch sides. Guamikeni also has a presence in Saint-Martin / Sint Maarten and Antigua, and serves the wider Caribbean.
