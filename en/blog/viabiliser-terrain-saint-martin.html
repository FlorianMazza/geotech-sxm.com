Site utilities & roadworks (VRD)

How do you service a plot of land in Saint-Martin?

Published on May 5, 2025 · Geotech Caraïbes

"Servicing" a plot means making it build-ready by bringing in the necessary access and utilities. It is an essential step before any villa, residential subdivision or building project in Saint-Martin. Here is how it works in practice.

The main stages of site servicing

  • Surveying and studies: defining the boundaries and investigating the soil;
  • Access and earthworks: creating the access roadway and the platform;
  • Bringing in utilities: drinking water, electricity, telecoms, drainage;
  • Connections: hookup to public networks or standalone systems.

Which utilities to bring in?

A serviced plot has, at a minimum, drinking water, electricity and a drainage solution (mains or on-site). Today, telecoms and fiber are added to this. Each utility requires a trench, a duct and a connection that complies with the utility operator's requirements.

The specifics of Saint-Martin / Sint Maarten

Depending on whether the plot is on the French or the Dutch side, the contacts, standards and utility operators differ. Good coordination with the network operators is essential to avoid delays. The topography and the nature of the soils also determine the route and cost of the work.

How long and what budget?

The duration and cost depend on the distance to existing networks, the nature of the soil (presence of rock) and the number of lots. A precise upfront assessment makes it possible to produce a reliable estimate and avoid cost overruns.
