OUR LOCAL ROOTS

Saint-Martin,
our ground.

Working in Saint-Martin can't be improvised. The soil, the climate, the logistical constraints and the island's administrative reality demand knowledge earned in the field — not from a textbook.

  • I.

    In-depth knowledge of the soil

    Volcanic ground, clay soils, coastal areas with a near-surface water table: every part of Saint-Martin has its own characteristics. Ten years of jobsites have allowed us to build a mental map of the island's soils that no outside player can reproduce. That knowledge feeds directly into our studies and our execution methodology.

  • II.

    Mastery of the island context

    Tropical climate, hurricane season from June to November, material supply constraints, the dual French-Dutch administration: we factor these realities in from the planning stage. Our jobsites are organized to absorb the challenges unique to Saint-Martin without compromising schedules or the quality of the works.

  • III.

    A local network

    Trusted local suppliers, utility operators, government offices, engineering firms, architects: Geotech Caraïbes relies on a solid professional fabric, patiently built over the years. This network is a resource we can mobilize directly for our clients — to speed up procedures and resolve complex situations.
