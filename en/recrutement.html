Project superintendent

Permanent · Management

Operational management of earthworks and site-utilities jobsites, crew coordination, schedule tracking, client and project-management relations, and progress reporting.

  • Jobsite planning and organization
  • Managing site foremen
  • Tracking budget, schedule, and quality
  • Client interface

Site foreman

Permanent · Supervisory

Leading the field crews, day-to-day organization of the jobsite, quality-of-execution control, safety, supply, and quantity tracking.

  • Directing the crews in the field
  • Reading and applying the plans
  • Enforcing safety rules
  • Inspecting completed work
Apply

Equipment operator

Permanent · Skilled worker

Operating hydraulic excavators, mini-excavators, compactors, and graders. Routine equipment maintenance, earthworks, platform, and utility-network work.

  • Operating Cat excavators and mini-excavators
  • Earthworks, topsoil stripping, compaction
  • First-level maintenance
  • Securing work areas
Apply

Heavy truck driver

Permanent · Skilled worker

Driving dump trucks to haul excavated material and supply materials. Jobsite delivery, load control, and vehicle maintenance.

  • Driving dump trucks on jobsites
  • Hauling and delivering materials
  • Routine vehicle maintenance
  • Compliance with traffic laws
Apply

Pipelayer

Permanent · Skilled worker

Laying and connecting wastewater, stormwater, and drinking-water piping. Trench work, manhole installation, tightness testing, and backfilling. Experience with buried utilities required.

  • Laying PVC / HDPE piping
  • Installing manholes and service connections
  • Tightness testing
  • Backfilling and compacting trenches
Apply

Site-utilities mason

Permanent · Skilled worker

Roadway masonry: setting curbs, gutters, manholes, and box culverts. Concrete work, masonry of small hydraulic structures and roadways, and asphalt laying.

  • Setting curbs and gutters
  • Masonry of manholes and structures
  • Hand-laying asphalt
  • Roadway finishing
Apply

Civil works laborer

Permanent / Fixed-term · Entry level welcome

Supporting the earthworks and site-utilities crews: material handling, traffic control, help with laying utilities, and clearing work areas. In-house training available for motivated candidates.

  • Assisting with earthworks
  • Jobsite signage and safety
  • Material handling and supply
  • Learning civil works techniques

Land surveyor

Topographic surveys, jobsite staking, control of execution grades, and as-built records. Proficiency with surveying equipment (total station, RTK GPS).

  • Jobsite surveys and staking
